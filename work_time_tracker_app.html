Instructions for live automated tracking via Messenger

Method 1: Direct Messenger Share (Built-in)

Click the "Send to Messenger" button after punching in/out. It automatically formats your timestamp and opens Messenger or copies the formatted line to paste into your staff group chat.

Method 2: Microsoft Forms Link via Messenger

1. Create a simple 2-question Microsoft Form ("Time In/Out" + "Employee Name").

2. Pin the Microsoft Form short link inside your Facebook Messenger group chat.

3. Responses sync instantly into your connected Excel workbook on OneDrive!
